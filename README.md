# 🏏 Virat Kohli — Century Legacy Dashboard

> **An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Virat Kohli's international batting performance, century trends, highest scores, opponents, formats, and playing conditions.**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The **Virat Kohli — Century Legacy Dashboard** transforms raw cricket match data into an interactive and visually engaging Power BI report.

The dashboard focuses on understanding Virat Kohli's century-making performance across **Test, ODI, and T20I cricket**, while highlighting important trends such as yearly performance, opponent-wise centuries, highest scores, and Home vs Away/Neutral performance.

The project follows an **insight-driven analytics approach** rather than simply presenting raw statistics.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

- **85 international centuries** highlight Virat Kohli's long-term consistency across formats.
- **254 runs** is the highest individual score captured in the dataset.
- **ODI cricket contributes the largest share of centuries with 54**, making it the strongest century-producing format.
- **Test cricket contributes 30 centuries**, demonstrating sustained performance in the longest format.
- The **Centuries by Year** analysis highlights changes in century production over time.
- **Opponent-wise analysis** identifies the teams against whom Kohli has produced the most centuries.
- The **Top 10 Highest Scores** visual highlights his most impactful individual innings.
- **Home vs Away/Neutral analysis** compares average scoring performance under different playing conditions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze Virat Kohli's century-making trends over time.
- Compare performance across **Test, ODI, and T20I** formats.
- Identify opponents against whom he has scored the most centuries.
- Highlight his highest individual scores.
- Compare average scoring performance across different playing conditions.
- Transform raw cricket statistics into meaningful and easy-to-understand insights.
- Demonstrate practical Power BI, DAX, and data visualization skills.

## 💡 Analytical Approach

This project follows a simple analytical framework:

**Raw Data → Transformation → Metrics → Visualization → Insights**

Instead of only displaying numbers, the dashboard focuses on identifying patterns and answering practical analytical questions such as:

> Which format contributes the most centuries?

> Which opponents have been the most favorable?

> How has century production changed over time?

> What are Kohli's highest individual scores?

> How does average scoring vary across playing conditions?
